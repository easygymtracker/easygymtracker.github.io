Privacy

A simple privacy posture: keep workout data on your own device.

This app is designed around local usage. Routines, sets, and session history are stored in the browser on the device you use, so you can manage training data without creating an account.

Home Features Open the app

What is stored

Routine definitions, set targets, exercise descriptions, session history, and body measurements (weight, body fat, and muscle) are all saved in browser storage on your device. Nothing is ever sent to a server.

What is not required

No user account, password, or remote profile is required to use the tracker.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, they are used for session-related reminders and workout flow while the app is running on your device.

Data portability

Export routines or body measurements to a portable JSON file at any time. Import files are processed locally — no data is uploaded to any server.

Your data stays private

The app has no backend, no sync service, and no user accounts. Every byte of your workout and body data remains only in your own browser storage.
