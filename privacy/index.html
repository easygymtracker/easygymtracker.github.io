Your privacy

No server, no tracking, no account — your workout data stays on your device.

Easy Gym Routine Tracker is built around a simple rule: nothing leaves your browser. Routines, sessions, body measurements, and history are stored locally and never sent anywhere.

Home Features Open the app

What is stored

Routine definitions, set targets, exercise descriptions, session history, and body measurements are all saved in browser storage on your device. Nothing is ever sent to a server.

No account required

No user account, email, password, or remote profile is needed. Open the app and start training — that's it.

No analytics or tracking scripts

The app loads zero third-party tracking scripts. There are no analytics services, no pixel trackers, and no advertising SDKs. Your usage is completely unmonitored.

No cookies

The app does not set any cookies. Your language preference and workout data are stored in browser localStorage, which is never sent to a server automatically.

Notifications stay local

If you allow notifications, they are generated entirely on your device for session-related reminders. No data is sent to a push server.

Data portability

Export routines or body measurements to a portable JSON file at any time. Import files are processed locally — no data is uploaded to any server.

Your data stays private

The app has no backend, no sync service, and no user accounts. Every byte of your workout and body data remains only in your own browser storage.

Get started

From first routine to personal records in four simple steps.

Easy Gym Routine Tracker keeps the workflow minimal: build once, train repeatedly, and watch your progress grow — all without leaving your phone.

Home Try it now See all features

1. Build your routine

Add exercises with target reps, weights, and rest periods. Supports both bilateral and unilateral (left/right) sets for full flexibility.

2. Start a session

Tap "Start" and work through each exercise set by set. Log what you actually lifted, get rest-timer alerts, and stay focused on the workout.

3. Review your progress

After each session, a summary shows total volume, reps, PRs, and fun comparisons. History charts let you track trends over weeks and months.

4. Stay in control

Export routines or body data to JSON at any time. Import them on another device or share them with a friend — no account or cloud service needed.

5. Install on your phone

Add the app to your home screen for instant access. It works offline and feels like a native app — no app-store download required.
